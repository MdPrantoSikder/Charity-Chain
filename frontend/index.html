Why CharityChain
You deserve to know
where your money goes.
Millions of donations are lost to fraud, inefficiency, and lack of accountability. We built CharityChain so you never have to wonder again.
🔗
Blockchain Verified
Every single donation is recorded on an immutable blockchain ledger. No one — not even us — can alter or delete a transaction once confirmed. Your proof is permanent.
🏦
Institutional Trustees
Only verified organizations — licensed banks, registered NGOs, and accredited hospitals — can approve fund releases. No anonymous approvals. No single point of trust.
📡
Real-Time Tracking
Track your donation from the moment you give to the moment it reaches the recipient. See exactly who verified it, which block it was recorded in, and when funds were released.
🛡️
Multi-Layer Verification
Every transaction passes through multiple independent validator nodes before confirmation. A single bad actor cannot approve or block any donation — consensus is required.
📁
Document Proof
Every case requires supporting documents — medical reports, identity proof, certificates. Documents are stored permanently on IPFS. Anyone can verify them anytime.
💵
Milestone Fund Release
Funds are not released all at once. They are distributed in verified milestones — ensuring money is used for what it was donated for, not misappropriated at any stage.

The Process
Simple for you. Rigorous behind the scenes.
You donate in seconds. Behind the scenes, a multi-layer verification process ensures your money is safe, verified, and tracked.
1
Browse Cases
Browse verified cases — each reviewed and approved by a licensed institution before going live.
2
You Donate
Choose an amount. Your donation is immediately locked in a secure escrow — protected until verified.
3
Network Verifies
Multiple independent validator nodes confirm your transaction. A block is created with a permanent cryptographic record.
4
Funds Released
Funds are released in verified milestones directly to the recipient. You receive a blockchain proof you can verify anytime.
